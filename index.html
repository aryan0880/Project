From Idea to Impact

NayePankh Foundation was born in 2019 in Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, from a simple but powerful belief: that students — energetic, passionate, and idealistic — could become the engine of social change in their own communities.

Founded by Prashant Shukla, the organisation started with a small group of friends who wanted to do more than just talk about India's challenges. They hit the streets, spoke to families in need, and launched their first food drive with whatever funds they could gather.

From those humble beginnings, NayePankh has grown into a registered entity recognised by the Government of Uttar Pradesh — with certification under 80G (donor tax exemption) and 12A (income tax exemption), making every rupee donated go further.

Today, we operate across Kanpur and Ghaziabad, supported by over 5,000 volunteers, and have touched more than 2 lakh lives. But we are just getting started.

What We're Fighting For

Four pillars of change, hundreds of drives, and thousands of lives transformed — here's how NayePankh Foundation creates real impact on the ground.

🍛 Food Security

No One Should
Sleep Hungry

Hunger is the most immediate injustice — and our food drives are our frontline response. Every week, NayePankh volunteers take to the streets of Kanpur and Ghaziabad with freshly prepared meals, rations, and dry groceries for families living below the poverty line.

From festival special drives to emergency relief, our food initiative has grown from a weekend project into a systematic programme that thousands of families depend on.

🍽 10,000+ Meals Served 📍 Kanpur & Ghaziabad 🗓 Weekly Drives 👥 500+ Families Served
🍛
📚 Education

Knowledge Is
the Greatest Gift

Education is the most powerful weapon against poverty. NayePankh runs mentorship sessions, distributes free stationery and books, and provides career guidance to children and young adults who cannot afford coaching or academic support.

Our student volunteers — many of them first-generation college-goers themselves — become relatable role models who inspire children to dream bigger and aim higher.

🎓 500+ Children Mentored 📖 Free Stationery Drives 🧭 Career Guidance Sessions 🏫 School Awareness Camps
📚
💊 Health & Hygiene

Dignity & Health
Are Basic Rights

Healthcare access in underserved communities remains a critical challenge. NayePankh bridges this gap through free health camps, medical awareness sessions, and targeted hygiene drives — with a special focus on women's menstrual health.

Our sanitary pad distribution drives have reached hundreds of women and girls who previously lacked access to basic menstrual hygiene products, breaking both the economic and social barriers around this crucial issue.

🩺 Free Health Camps 🌸 Sanitary Pad Drives 💉 Awareness Sessions 🏥 Medical Guidance
💊
👕 Clothing Drives

Warmth Is
a Human Right

For many families living on the streets or in temporary shelters, winter is a life-threatening season. NayePankh's clothing drives — with priority given to winter garments — provide blankets, sweaters, and warm clothing to those most vulnerable to the cold.

We run community collection drives throughout the year, sorting, cleaning, and distributing donated garments with care and dignity — because how we give matters as much as what we give.

🧥 Winter Priority Drives 🧣 Blankets & Warm Wear 📦 Community Collections 🤝 Dignity-First Approach
